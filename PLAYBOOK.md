# The Playbook: Stream Clips → Money

## 0. Two lanes, kept on SEPARATE accounts

| | **Lane A: Campaign clipping** | **Lane B: Your own GTA 6 page** (tips, tricks, secrets, news) |
|---|---|---|
| Footage | Streamers' streams (only campaign streamers) | **Your own gameplay**, recorded by your brother |
| Who pays | The streamer/brand via Content Rewards / Whop, per 1k views | **The platforms**: YouTube Shorts revenue, TikTok Creator Rewards, IG/FB bonuses, plus affiliate links and brand deals |
| Rate | ~$0.75–1.50 per 1k views (often capped, nothing under ~5k views) | YouTube Shorts ~$0.05–0.40 per 1k (higher for UK/US viewers); TikTok Creator Rewards ~$0.40–1 per 1k on videos **over 1 minute** |
| Unlock | Nothing: post and get paid | YouTube: 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days (then apply, about a month). TikTok: 18+, 10k followers, 100k views in 30 days |
| Speed of money | **Days–weeks** | **Weeks–months**, but it **compounds** and keeps paying after GTA hype and campaign budgets fade |
| Tools | Live clipper → approval queue → publisher | Trend radar briefs → film → same editor/captions |
| Accounts | Campaign account sets (e.g. "GTA Clips Daily") | One brand: e.g. "Leonida Tips" on YouTube + TikTok + IG |

**Why separate:** a channel full of streamer clips gets rejected from YouTube's Partner Program for
"reused content". Keep your own-footage page clean so it qualifies for platform pay.

**How the two feed each other:** Lane A pays the bills now and teaches us which hooks and topics win.
Lane B turns that knowledge into a page you own, and that page is what reaches a steady £10k a month.

## 1. Where the money actually comes from (in order of speed)

| # | Source | How you get paid | When money can arrive | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paid clipping campaigns** (Content Rewards on Whop, Vyro, ClipAffiliates, streamer Discords) | Streamer/brand pays per 1,000 verified views on clips you post | **Days–weeks** after views are approved | median ~$1 / 1k views; YouTube campaigns ~$1.40 / 1k |
| 2 | **Retainers**: you clip *for* a streamer, posted on their channel | Monthly invoice | When the client pays you | £500–£2,500 / month per streamer |
| 3 | **YouTube Shorts ad revenue** (Partner Program) | Google AdSense, monthly | **Months.** You must qualify first | ~$0.40 / 1k engaged views after the 45% split |

**Key fact:** for your first £1,000, #1 is the only route that can pay in weeks. YouTube ad money
is a long-term bonus, not the plan. Example: 1M Shorts views ≈ $405 from YouTube, vs ≈ $1,000–1,400 from a campaign.

### How YouTube pays you (once you're in the Partner Program)
1. **Qualify**: 1,000 subscribers **and** 10M public Shorts views in the last 90 days (or 4,000 long-form watch hours).
   YouTube is tightening this for *new* channels: from **1 Feb 2027**, Shorts revenue requires 10M views every 90 days.
   Check the current rules in YouTube Studio → Earn before relying on them.
2. **Apply** in YouTube Studio → Earn. Review takes ~1 month. Clip channels are often rejected for
   **"reused content"**, so add your own value: hook text, captions, edits, and permission from the streamer.
3. **Link an AdSense for YouTube account** (one per person; it can serve all your channels). Add bank details and the
   US tax form (W-8BEN for UK residents; it lowers US withholding).
4. **Payment cycle**: earnings for month X are finalised around the 7th–12th of month X+1 and paid around the
   20th–26th, **only if your balance is ≥ £60**; otherwise it rolls over.

### How campaign platforms pay you
1. Join a campaign → post the clip on your account (YouTube/TikTok/IG) → submit the post link in the campaign.
2. The streamer/brand approves the clip; the platform counts verified views (often capped per clip and with a minimum).
3. Earnings go to your platform balance (e.g. Whop) → withdraw to your bank/PayPal. Fees ~10% on some.
4. Read every brief: required tags, caption text, which platforms count, max payout per clip, deadlines.

**UK tax:** this is self-employment income. Register for Self Assessment with HMRC once you earn
over £1,000 a year (trading allowance). Keep `tracker.csv` and payout receipts.

## 2. The plan to the first £1,000

**Maths:** £1,000 ≈ $1,300 ÷ ~$1.20 per 1k (after fees) ≈ **1.1M paid views**.
Shorts views follow a "hit" pattern: most clips get 200–2,000 views and one in 20–50 gets 50k–1M.
So the job is **volume × quality** → **~150–300 clips** in the first month.

### Week 1: set up (days 1–3), then post
- [ ] Sign up on Content Rewards (Whop) + 1–2 other clipping marketplaces. Filter for **streamer/creator campaigns** that
      pay on **YouTube Shorts**, ≥ $1/1k, with no tiny per-clip caps. Pick **3–5 campaigns** with streamers who stream daily.
- [ ] Add each to `streamers.md` with its rules.
- [ ] Create **one YouTube channel per streamer/campaign** (brand accounts under one Google account, e.g.
      "<Streamer> Daily Clips"). Same channel names on TikTok + Instagram.
- [ ] Phone-verify each YouTube channel (unlocks custom thumbnails and longer uploads).
- [ ] Every day: send me the newest VOD per streamer → I return 6–12 finished clips each.

### Posting rules
- **2–4 Shorts per channel per day**, spaced 3–4 h apart (schedule them in YouTube Studio). ~10–20/day across all channels.
- **Same clip → that streamer's YouTube + TikTok + IG**. Never post the same clip on two of *your* YouTube channels (spam policy).
- Post within **24 h of the stream**. Fresh moments win, and streamer fanbases search for them.
- Title = streamer name + emotion/curiosity. Use the description file I generate (includes credit).
- Don't delete flops; do double down on the *type* of moment that hits (I'll learn it from the tracker).

### Weeks 2–4: optimise
- Every 3–4 days, fill in `views_7d` in `tracker.csv` → I analyse which clip types, lengths, hooks win and adjust picks.
- Drop campaigns where clips don't get approved or views stall; add new ones.
- Once a channel has a hit clip, use it as **proof** to pitch that streamer (or others) for a monthly retainer.

### Realistic expectations
- Paid campaign money **can** start in week 2, but the first £1,000 most likely lands in **weeks 3–8**,
  depending on hits. Nobody can guarantee views.
- The way to £10k/month is campaigns (volume) + 3–6 retainers (stable). YouTube ad revenue comes on top later.

## 3. Daily routine (≈ 1–2 hours of your time)
1. Morning: "Clip the latest streams for <streamer A>, <B>, <C>" → I analyse, pick, render, QA, send files.
2. Skim the clips (30 s each), delete any you don't like.
3. Upload + schedule on YouTube / TikTok / IG; paste title + description from the `.txt`.
4. Submit post links to each campaign; log in `tracker.csv`.
5. Every few days: update views and ask me "what's working?"
